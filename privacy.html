Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

FMU Studio makes AI social adverts and edits video for brands. FMU Studio is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us about anything on this page at hello@fmustudio.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Booking a call. When you book through our calendar (run by Cal.com) we receive your name, email address and anything you add to the booking. We use it to hold the call and follow up about your project, because you asked us to.
  • Emails with us. If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and do the work. We may keep it for as long as it's useful to the working relationship.
  • Business outreach. We sometimes contact people at companies whose work fits ours. For this we hold a business contact's name, work email, job title and company, taken from public business sources such as company websites and professional profiles. We do this under our legitimate interest in finding clients, and we only contact people about work relevant to their role. Every email tells you how to opt out. Reply "no thanks" or email us and we'll stop and add you to a do-not-contact list so you aren't contacted again.
  • Client work. Files and details you share for a project are used only to deliver that project.

We don't sell personal data, and this website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Embedded content

Our portfolio videos play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and our booking calendar loads from Cal.com. These services may set their own cookies when you play a video or use the calendar, under their own privacy policies.

Who handles data for us

We use trusted providers to run the business: Netlify (website hosting), Supabase (our contact database, stored in London), Zoho and Google (email), Cal.com (booking calendar), and freelance editors who work on client projects under confidentiality. Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

Outreach contacts who don't reply are removed within 12 months of our last email. Opt-outs are kept on our do-not-contact list so we can respect them. Client and booking records are kept for as long as needed for the work and for our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to us using it, including for outreach. Email hello@fmustudio.co.uk and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
